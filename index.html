Pulsii is one public canvas. An accepted tap becomes one fading pulse on each healthy canvas that stays connected through its batch, including yours. If Pulsii cannot accept it, it says so rather than showing a private copy.

No account, name, message, or canvas history. This is not a private room. Pulsii records aggregate operational counters without pulse positions, colours, or identifiers; the hosting provider may also keep ordinary connection logs.

Calm visuals start on with smaller, dimmer pulses. Dense crowds automatically use small, non-additive halos and a brightness ceiling without imposing a fixed pulse cap. Pause hides pulse visuals immediately.
