# Pulsii preliminary name-clearance note

Checked 2 August 2026. This is a launch-risk screen, not legal advice or trade
mark clearance.

## Finding

Treat **Pulsii** as a provisional product name for the isolated review and
controlled beta. Do not yet invest in public promotion, paid acquisition, a new
visual identity, merchandise, or a trade mark application under this name.

The current risk assessment is:

| Risk | Assessment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Search and discoverability | Medium–high | Two active exact-name commercial uses already occupy the most obvious domains |
| Brand confusion | Medium–high | One exact-name business uses closely related ideas of pulse, rhythm, connection, and sharing |
| UK legal conflict | Unknown | The official UK register still needs a manual exact, similar, and class-specific search |
| Private review use | Lower, not cleared | The planned preview is unindexed, unadvertised, temporary, and described as a restoration review |

## Exact-name commercial uses found

### `pulsii.com`

[`pulsii.com`](https://pulsii.com/) is an active apparel and music-oriented
shop. It sells clothing under the Pulsii name and uses music-focused branding.
This creates an exact-name search and potential future merchandise overlap even
if the current web experiment is a different service.

### `pulsii.com.sg`

[`pulsii.com.sg`](https://pulsii.com.sg/) is an active Singapore restaurant.
Its own brand explanation describes Pulsii through pulse, energy, rhythm, life,
connection, and sharing. That semantic territory is unusually close to this
project's "one shared moment" positioning, despite the very different service
category.

The official restaurant site is sufficient evidence of active commercial use;
a registry extract should be obtained only if a legal review requires it.

## What this search did not establish

- It did not establish that either business owns a conflicting UK, EU, or
  international trade mark.
- It did not establish that Pulsii is unavailable for software or entertainment
  services.
- It did not establish that no similar-sounding mark exists.
- It did not complete class-specific searches of the UK IPO, EUIPO, or WIPO
  registers. Their interactive search results were not reliably accessible in
  this automated pass.
- The absence of a clearly indexed exact-name software product in broad web and
  app-store searches is weak negative evidence and should not be treated as
  clearance.

The [UK Intellectual Property Office search
guide](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark) directs applicants to search by
keyword, phrase, owner, and image. The [WIPO Global Brand
Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) covers many
national and international collections, but WIPO itself recommends also
checking national and regional registers and considering professional advice.

## Manual clearance sequence

Before broad public distribution:

1. Search the UK IPO register for `Pulsii`, `Pulsi`, `Pulsie`, `Pulsey`, and
   close phonetic variants.
2. Repeat in EUIPO and WIPO, including live and pending marks.
3. Review candidate service areas rather than exact spelling alone. Likely areas
   to investigate include downloadable software, communications, entertainment,
   and hosted software services; a professional should confirm the relevant
   Nice classes before any filing.
4. Record each plausible mark's owner, territory, status, filing date, classes,
   and goods/services wording.
5. Recheck Companies House, app stores, major social platforms, and the wider
   web for passing-off and discoverability risks that trade mark registers do
   not answer.
6. Decide either to keep and qualify Pulsii or rename before public launch.

Do not buy domains, handles, searches, reports, or legal services as part of
this checklist without a separate spending decision.

## Recommended decision boundary

- Keep the repository and restoration branch named Pulsii.
- It is reasonable to use the generated, unindexed review hostname for an
  invited technical test.
- Do not attach the production domain, enable indexing, submit to launch
  channels, or spend on promotion until the user has reviewed the official
  register results and explicitly accepted the naming risk.
- If a rename is likely, do it before final screenshots, videos, sponsor
  material, or public launch copy are produced.
