# Controlled beta checklist

Use this for one coordinated session with 8–15 invited participants. It records
aggregate observations, not names, IP addresses, pulse locations, colours, or
individual histories.

## Before the session

- Confirm the preview is the isolated restoration service.
- Warm the free service and wait for `/healthz` to respond.
- Confirm crawling remains disabled.
- Open one iPad Safari client and one second-browser client.
- Confirm pause, reduced motion, share/copy, About, and reconnect behaviour.
- Tell invitees it is one public shared canvas, not a private room.
- Tell invitees that pulses are visual, fade, and have no canvas history.
- Provide a stop time and a route for private feedback.
- Do not invite anyone who has not agreed to view animated output.

## Questions after the session

1. What did you think Pulsii was within the first ten seconds?
2. Could you tell when another connection was present?
3. Did receiving a pulse make you respond?
4. Was anything confusing, inaccessible, uncomfortable, or too intense?
5. Would you invite one person into it for a minute?
6. Is the experience more interesting as a scheduled moment, a private room, an
   event canvas, or none of those?
7. Would you use a paid private or branded version? For what occasion?

## Decision thresholds

These are product hypotheses, not industry benchmarks:

- At least 80% send a first pulse within ten seconds.
- At least 70% receive a remote pulse within 30 seconds.
- At least 40% visibly reciprocate within ten seconds.
- No duplicate pulse, critical reconnect, or serious iPad failure.
- No safety complaint or unexpectedly flash-like output.
- At least three participants say they would invite someone, or one organiser
  describes a credible event/private-room use.

If the safety, exact-once, or reconnect gate fails, stop distribution and fix it.
If comprehension succeeds but overlap is weak, test scheduled sessions before
adding more features. If nobody wants to invite another person, do not buy
traffic.
