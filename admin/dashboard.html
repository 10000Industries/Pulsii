Counts are anonymous aggregates. They include your own use, reloads, reconnects and automated traffic. There is no reliable unique-person or returning-visitor count. Historical coverage begins when recording is enabled; gaps are not proof of zero usage. Closed-connection statistics are grouped by their closing time and may include connections started before the selected period. History is retained for 90 days; export CSV for longer records.
